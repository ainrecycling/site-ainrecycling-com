Last updated: 7 November 2026

This translation is provided for information only: only the French version is legally binding.

1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of the ainrecycling.com website, published by AIN RECYCLING (see the legal notice), and the purchase of tungsten carbide offered by Ain Recycling, in person or by parcel. Using the site or selling us material means you accept these terms.

2. Access to the site

The site is free and accessible without creating an account. We do our best to keep it available, but it may be interrupted, in particular for maintenance.

3. Information and prices shown

  • The prices per kilo shown on the site are today's prices. For carbide inserts, drills and end mills: 47 € per kilo by parcel; at the depot and on collection rounds, the price is confirmed before the appointment. They follow the tungsten market and may change.
  • The price of the day of your parcel request is guaranteed for 14 days: for a parcel sent within that time, that price is paid, even if the market falls.
  • The applicable price per kilo is confirmed to you before purchase: before weighing, or when you request to sell by parcel. An estimate made from photos is binding on neither you nor us.
  • The final amount is calculated on the weight measured at weighing and on the material actually received, after sorting.
  • Blocks, rollers and any part other than carbide inserts, drills and end mills, as well as collection rounds and pick-ups, are priced on request.

4. Purchase conditions

Who can sell

  • The seller must be an adult, or be represented by their legal representative.
  • The seller declares on their honour that they own the material and acquired it lawfully.
  • A private individual shows a valid ID. For parcel shipments, it is only requested for the first shipment.
  • A business provides its company number (SIRET in France).

Payment

  • Payment is made only by bank transfer or cheque. Cash payment is prohibited when buying metals (Article L112-6 of the French Monetary and Financial Code).
  • Each purchase gives rise to a numbered purchase receipt, sent by email, itemising weights and prices.

Materials

  • We buy tungsten carbide inserts, drills and end mills. Other carbide parts (blocks, rollers…) are assessed on request.
  • We may refuse material that is not tungsten carbide, or whose origin seems doubtful.

Purchase by parcel

  • Parcel shipping is reserved for shipments from metropolitan France. From another country, contact us before sending anything.
  • Materials accepted by parcel: carbide inserts, drills and end mills. Blocks, rollers and other parts require our prior agreement.
  • Shipping is paid by Ain Recycling: we email the seller a prepaid label for each parcel.
  • Each parcel weighs 20 kg at most. Above that, the seller splits the material into several parcels.
  • Liability during transport: to be defined.
  • On receipt, the parcel is sorted and weighed. The seller receives their purchase receipt by email.
  • Payment is made within 48 hours (working days) of weighing, by bank transfer or cheque. A photo of the scale with the batch is sent with the purchase receipt.
  • If you disagree with the amount: to be defined.

Collection rounds and pick-ups

  • For large volumes, in France and across Europe, we can come and collect the material. The price and collection date are agreed together, case by case, before the pick-up.
