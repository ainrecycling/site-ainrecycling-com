Cookie policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This translation is provided for information only: only the French version is legally binding.

In short: this site uses no trackers. Only the customer area sets a strictly necessary login cookie when you log in. That is why no banner asks for your consent.

What is a cookie?

A cookie (or "tracker") is a small file or piece of information stored on your device by a website, for example to measure audience, show advertising or remember your choices.

What our site does

  • No cookies are set during your visit, either by us or by any external service.
  • The only exception: the customer area. When you log in, a session cookie ("espace") keeps you logged in for up to 60 days. It is deleted when you log out and is used for nothing else.
  • No audience measurement (no Google Analytics or similar tool) and no advertising.
  • No external content is loaded: no embedded videos, no social media buttons, no online maps. The site's fonts and programs are hosted on our own site.
  • The WhatsApp button is a plain link: it loads nothing and sets no tracker unless you click it.
  • Apart from this login cookie, nothing is stored in your browser (no "local storage").
  • Forms stay in your browser until you send them. In the customer area, ID photos are reduced on your device before being sent.

Why no consent banner?

The law (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act) requires your consent before setting trackers that are not strictly necessary for the site to work. Our site's only cookie, the customer area's, is strictly necessary for the login you ask for: it does not need your consent. So there is nothing to accept or refuse.

Links to other sites

Some links open another website (for example the CNIL's), WhatsApp or your email app. These services have their own cookie rules.

If this changes

If we ever add a tool that uses trackers, we will update this page and ask for your consent beforehand where the law requires it.
