Cookie policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This translation is provided for information only: only the French version is legally binding.

In short: one measurement cookie, no advertising. It counts our visitors, for us alone. You can ask not to be counted, further down this page. No outside service.

What is a cookie?

A cookie (or "tracker") is a small file or piece of information stored on your device by a website, for example to measure audience, show advertising or remember your choices.

What our site does

  • Visit measurement: a “visite” cookie remembers the date of your first and your last visit, for 13 months at most. It only tells us whether a visitor is new or returning. It contains no name and no identifier, is read only by our site and is used for nothing else: French law exempts this kind of measurement from prior consent.
  • Your choice: if you ask not to be counted, this choice is kept in your browser. Once closed, the information banner does not come back.
  • Your account: when you sign in, a session cookie (“espace”) keeps you signed in for 60 days at most; it is deleted when you sign out. Your first name and the draft of a request you have started stay in your browser, so that you do not have to type everything again.
  • Convenience: a notice you have closed, or a language you have declined, is not offered to you again.
  • No Google Analytics or similar tool, and no advertising. The site only counts its own page views: the page read and the site you came from, without cookies and without anything that could identify you.
  • No external content is loaded: no embedded videos, no social media buttons, no online maps. The site's fonts and programs are hosted on our own site.
  • The WhatsApp button is a plain link: it loads nothing and sets no tracker unless you click it.
  • Forms stay in your browser until you send them. In your account, ID photos are reduced on your device before being sent.

Not being counted

You can ask at any time to stop being counted among our visitors. It changes nothing about how you use the site.

Links to other sites

Some links open another website (for example the CNIL's), WhatsApp or your email app. These services have their own cookie rules.

If this changes

If we ever add a tool that uses trackers, we will update this page and ask for your consent beforehand where the law requires it.

A question?

Email us at contact@ainrecycling.com. To find out more about your data, see our privacy policy.
