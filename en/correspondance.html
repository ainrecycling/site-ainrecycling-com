Packing

Carbide is heavy: pack it securely.

  • 1

    Closed containers

    Put inserts and tools in plastic bottles or thick bags, firmly closed.

  • 2

    Strong box

    A double-wall box, padded with paper so nothing moves.

  • 3

    Tape everywhere

    Tape all edges generously: a heavy parcel easily bursts open.

  • 4

    20 kg maximum

    Over 20 kg, make several parcels: each one gets its own prepaid label.
