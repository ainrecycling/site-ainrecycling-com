The company

A company from the Ain, buying by the rules.

Ain Recycling is a company based in Port, in the Ain department (France), specialising in the tungsten carbide trade. We buy across France and Europe. Buying metals from private individuals is regulated by law: we apply these rules to every transaction.

  • No cash payments

    French law prohibits it when buying metals. Bank transfer or cheque: a traceable payment that protects you too.

  • Identity checked, data protected

    Private individuals must show an ID. It is kept private and never shared.

  • A receipt for every sale

    Numbered, dated, with weights and price per kilo itemised: a clear record, sent by email.

  • Transparent weighing

    The price per kilo is set before weighing. You know exactly what you are selling, and at what price.

We buy tungsten carbide from private individuals and businesses: at our depot in the Ain department, by parcel from France, and on collection rounds across Europe.
