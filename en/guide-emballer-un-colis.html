Packing a 20 kg parcel

Carbide is heavy: 20 kg fit in a small box, and a badly sealed parcel opens in transit. Here is how to pack it securely.

What you need

  • A small, sturdy double-wall box.
  • Thick plastic bottles, tubs or doubled freezer bags.
  • Newspaper or kraft paper for padding.
  • Wide packing tape.

The steps

  1. Put inserts and tools in tightly closed containers.
  2. Place them at the bottom of the box, up to 20 kg.
  3. Pad with paper so that nothing moves.
  4. Seal every edge with tape, top and bottom.
  5. Stick the prepaid label on flat and put the shipping slip inside the parcel.
  6. Drop the parcel off at the point shown on the label and keep the proof of postage until you are paid.

What to avoid

  • A plastic bag on its own: it tears.
  • An old, worn-out box: it splits under the weight.
  • More than 20 kg in a single parcel: make two.
  • Bare pointed tools: they pierce the box.
