How much is a kilo of carbide worth?

A kilo of used carbide sells for several tens of euros, far more than steel. With us, today's price by parcel is 47 €/kg for inserts, drills and end mills. Here is what sets that price.

By Malik Gülseven, updated on 9 November 2026

In short

  • With us: 47 €/kg by parcel, free label.
  • The price follows the tungsten market.
  • Keep your carbide separate from steel.

What sets the price

1. The tungsten price

Tungsten carbide usually contains between 70 and 90% tungsten, a metal whose price is tracked by the metal markets. When that price rises, the buying price rises; when it falls, it falls.

2. The material

Inserts and solid carbide drills and end mills are almost entirely carbide: they have the same price. Brazed tools and parts mixed with steel are worth less, because only the carbide share counts.

3. How clean the batch is

Oil, swarf, screws and holders are not carbide: they are removed during sorting. A clean batch is paid for at its real weight.

4. Quantity and location

By parcel, the price per kilo is the same from 2 to 40 kg. For large volumes, the price is set by quote, with a collection.

Why not the price of steel?

Mixed with steel swarf, carbide goes at the scrap metal price. Kept apart and sold to a specialist, it is paid at the carbide price, which is much higher.

How to get the best price

  • Separate carbide and steel: the magnet test is enough.
  • Remove screws, clamps and holders.
  • Drain off the oil, without washing.
  • Group your shipments: at least 2 kg per parcel.
  • Compare with a published, dated price, not with an estimate over the phone.

Our price is published, dated and guaranteed for 7 days after your request, until the end of the month at the latest. The label is free, with no sorting or handling fees.
