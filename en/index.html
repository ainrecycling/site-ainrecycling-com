Recycling

Carbide isn't thrown away: it's recycled.

The carbide you sell us goes into the recycling chain, where the tungsten is recovered to make new tools.

  • ≈ 2×denser than steel: its weight is the easiest way to recognise it.
  • Wtungsten, recovered from used carbide, is used to make new carbide tools.
