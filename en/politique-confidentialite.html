Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This translation is provided for information only: only the French version is legally binding.

This page explains, in plain terms, what personal data Ain Recycling uses, why, for how long and with whom, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés).

1. Who is responsible for your data?

AIN RECYCLING, a simplified joint-stock company with a single shareholder under French law (RCS Bourg-en-Bresse 132 041 039), 1 rue des Prairies, 01460 Port, France. Contact for any question about your data: contact@ainrecycling.com.

2. What this site does not do

  • No advertising, no outside measurement service. The site counts its own page views, with nothing that could identify you. Only with your consent, a “visite” cookie tells us whether you have been here before.
  • No external service is loaded during your visit: the site's fonts and programs are hosted on our own site. The WhatsApp button is a plain link: nothing is sent to WhatsApp unless you click it.
  • In your browser, the site keeps only what it needs to work: your cookie choice, the first name on your account, the draft of a request you have started. The details are in the cookie policy.
  • No automated decision-making or profiling.
  • Your data is never sold or rented.

4. Who receives your data?

Only authorised people at Ain Recycling have access. We use technical service providers, who only use your data on our behalf:

  • OVHcloud (OVH SAS, France): hosting of the website and customer accounts, on servers located in France, and domain name.
  • Vercel Inc. (United States): hosting of our internal purchase management tool.
  • Supabase: database and private storage for purchase files, on servers located in Paris.
  • Resend: sending emails (purchase receipts, account login codes, price alert), from the European Union (Ireland).
  • The French government's company register (recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr): receives from our server only the text typed in the "Company" field of the business forms, and nothing else about you.
  • Google: the company's mailbox (Gmail), which receives your replies and the internal copies of purchase receipts; and the “Continue with Google” sign-in, if you choose it.
  • The carrier that delivers your parcel with the prepaid label we send you: the sender's name, address and, if needed, phone number, only to collect and deliver the parcel.

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, your messages go through that service, operated by WhatsApp Ireland Ltd (Meta group), which processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

The purchase register and purchase files may be shown to the authorities (police, gendarmerie, tax and customs authorities) when they request them within the law.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are US companies. Where data can be accessed from the United States, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) for companies that have joined it, such as Vercel and Meta, or otherwise by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Security

  • The site and our tools only work over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), which your browser is required to use, and our pages can only load files from our own site.
  • ID documents are kept in private storage, accessible only to authorised accounts.
  • An ID document is never emailed to the seller and is never accessible via a public link.
  • My account: login with a one-time code or link sent by email, without a password. ID photos are reduced on your device, then sent over an encrypted connection to private storage, where they stay encrypted, like the ID number and your IBAN: only you (logged in) and Ain Recycling can see them.

7. Your rights

You can at any time ask to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its use or receive it in a portable format, or object to its use based on our legitimate interest. You can also set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

Email us at contact@ainrecycling.com. We reply within one month. Some data cannot be erased before the end of the period required by law (for example purchase receipts).

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: cnil.fr/fr/plaintes, or by post: CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France. If you live in another European Union country, you can also contact the data protection authority of that country.

8. Minors

We only buy from adults. A minor can only sell through their legal representative.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, for example if a new service is added to the site. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
