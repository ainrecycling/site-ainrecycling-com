Price

What is your price per kilo?

For carbide inserts, drills and end mills: 47 € per kilo by parcel, shipping paid by us (prepaid label), or 50 € per kilo delivered to our depot in the Ain, by appointment. This price of the day follows the tungsten market and is guaranteed for 7 days after your request, until the end of the month at the latest. On a collection round, it is confirmed to you before we come. Blocks, rollers and other parts: price on request.

What if I disagree with the weight?

Your parcel is weighed on camera, first closed, then the carbide alone. We tell you the weight and the amount before paying: nothing is final until you have confirmed. If the weight surprises you, ask us for the video: we go through it with you. If we cannot agree, we send your lot back. Once you have confirmed, the sale is final. The details are in our terms of sale (in French).

Material

I am clearing out a workshop (retirement, inheritance, closure): what should I do with the carbide?

Don't throw it away: carbide inserts, drills, end mills and parts sell by the kilo, even worn or broken. New tools in their box are often worth more resold as tools. For the rest, send us a photo on WhatsApp. It is all explained on the page clearing out a workshop?

How can I tell if my tool is carbide?

Carbide is very heavy for its size, matt grey (or coated black, gold or bluish) and does not rust. Unlike steel, it is only weakly attracted by a magnet, or not at all. The easiest way: send us a photo.

Do you buy parts other than inserts, drills and end mills?

Yes, blocks, rollers and other carbide parts are assessed case by case, with a price on request. Send us a photo and a rough weight.

Do you buy brazed tools?

Case by case: on a brazed tool, only the tip is carbide, brazed onto a steel body. Send us a photo: we will tell you whether we take it, and at what price.

My carbide is oily or dirty: should I clean it?

No need to wash it: let it drain well and remove swarf and steel parts. Oil and swarf are not carbide: the price per kilo applies to the carbide alone.

Shipping

Can I sell by parcel?

Yes, from anywhere in mainland France. Make your request on the private sellers page: we send you a prepaid label (shipping paid by us, 20 kg maximum per parcel). Your parcel is weighed on receipt, then you receive your purchase receipt and your payment.

Is there a minimum weight?

For a parcel, we advise you to wait until you have at least 2 kg: keep your inserts and broken tools in a jar and send it when it is full. One parcel, one label.

My parcel arrived damaged: what happens?

If the parcel arrives damaged or incomplete, we let you know as soon as it arrives, with photos. Keep your proof of postage until you are paid: that is what counts with the carrier. The best thing is still to pack it securely: see our packing tips.

Can you collect my carbide?

Yes, for large volumes: we organise collection rounds and pick-ups in France and across Europe. The price is set on request, depending on quantity and location. Contact us by email or on WhatsApp.

I live outside France. How can I sell?

Sending by parcel is reserved for mainland France. From another European country, contact us by email or WhatsApp: we look at how to take back your carbide, for example on a collection round. From Switzerland or a country outside the European Union, customs formalities are required: contact us before sending anything.

Payment

When do I get paid?

Within 48 hours (working days) of your agreement on the weight, by bank transfer or cheque, as you prefer. You first receive the weight, the price per kilo and the amount by email: you confirm, and the payment is sent.

Why don't you pay cash?

Because French law prohibits it when buying metals. Bank transfers and cheques also protect the seller: the payment is traceable.

Businesses

We are a business: what documents will we receive?

A numbered purchase receipt for every sale: date, type of material, weight, price per kilo, amount, our numbers (SIRET, VAT) and the VAT reverse-charge statement. On request, a take-back certificate for your waste register. See also our Businesses page.

How does the VAT reverse charge work?

Between VAT-registered companies in France, the sale of waste and recovered materials falls under the reverse charge (article 283-2 sexies of the French General Tax Code): you sell excluding VAT, and the buyer, Ain Recycling, accounts for the VAT. The statement appears on the purchase receipt.

Can you give me a certificate for my waste register?

Yes, on request: a take-back certificate listing the dates, weights and type of material, with our numbers (SIRET, VAT). The purchase receipt for each sale already contains this information.

Data

Do you buy from private individuals?

Yes, from adults. You just need a valid ID and an email address to receive the purchase receipt. Businesses give us their company number (SIRET in France).

Why create an account?

So that you only send us your ID once. It is kept privately, together with your contact details and bank details: your next sales take just a few clicks. Everything happens in your account.

What happens to my data?

It is only used to issue the purchase receipt and to meet our legal obligations. It is never sold, and the site uses no trackers. Everything is explained in our privacy policy.
