How it works

Three steps, with no complicated formalities.

  • 1. A photo and the weight

    By email or on WhatsApp: the material, a rough weight and your town.

  • 2. The price and the date

    Price confirmed in writing and guaranteed for 7 days (within the month), collection grouped with our next round.

  • 3. Weighing and payment

    Weighed in front of you, purchase receipt with our numbers (VAT no. FR58132041039), bank transfer within 48 h.

What we buy

The same carbide, on both sides of the Rhine.

We buy

  • Carbide inserts, coated or not
  • Solid carbide drills, end mills, reamers and taps
  • Rods, blanks and wear parts (priced case by case)

We do not buy

  • High-speed steel (HSS) tools
  • Steel holders, bodies and tapers
  • Ceramic and cermet inserts
