What we buy

FAQ

Questions about drills and end mills.

Is a broken drill bought?

Yes, whole or broken, at the same price per kilo: only the weight of carbide counts.

How do I know if my drill is carbide?

It is much heavier than a steel drill of the same size and hardly sticks to a magnet. The markings “HM”, “VHM” or “carbide” confirm it. See our 3-test guide.

Should I remove anything before sending?

Only what is not carbide: steel chucks, collets, tool holders and rings. Solid carbide tools are sold whole.

Do you buy brazed tools?

Case by case: on a brazed tool, only the tip is carbide, brazed onto a steel body. Send us a photo: we will tell you whether we take it, and at what price.
