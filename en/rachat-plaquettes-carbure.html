FAQ

Questions about inserts.

Do I need to sort inserts by shape or grade?

No: all carbide inserts have the same price per kilo. Just set aside anything that is not carbide (screws, holders, ceramic inserts).

Are new inserts paid more?

No, they are bought at the same price per kilo as worn inserts: the carbide is recycled either way.

How do I recognise a ceramic or cermet insert?

It has the same shape and ISO codes as a carbide insert, but it is much lighter. If in doubt, send us a photo.

Is there a minimum weight?

For a parcel, we advise you to wait until you have at least 2 kg: keep your inserts and broken tools in a jar and send it when it is full. One parcel, one label.
