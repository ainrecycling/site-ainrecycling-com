Clearing out a workshop?

Worn tools, loose inserts, odds and ends from the drawer: carbide sells by the kilo. Here is what has value, our honest advice, and how we take care of the rest.

What has value

  • Turning and milling inserts, even worn or broken.
  • Solid carbide drills, end mills and taps.
  • Carbide rods, offcuts, dies and wear parts.
  • Brazed tools (a carbide tip on a steel body): assessed case by case.

No need to sort everything: a mix of inserts and tools is bought too. Just set aside steel, high-speed steel (HSS) and ceramics, which are not carbide. Not sure? See recognising carbide in 3 tests.

Our honest advice: new tools

New tools, still in their box? Resold as tools, to another workshop or on a classified ads site, they are often worth more than by the kilo. Do it if you have the time: we buy everything else, worn, broken or odd pieces.

How it works

  1. Send us a photo of the batch on WhatsApp, with the rough weight: we tell you what we buy.
  2. Up to 20 kg per parcel: we send you a prepaid label, shipping is paid by us.
  3. A large volume: request a quote, answer within 24 h (working days), then we come and collect it by appointment.
  4. Filmed weighing, detailed purchase receipt, then payment within 48 h (working days), by bank transfer or cheque.

For an inherited workshop, the purchase receipt is made out in the name of the person selling, with their ID, as for any seller.

A company closing down?

The sale is made in the company's name, with a detailed purchase document (weight, price per kilo, amount) and payment to its account: enough to justify the sale to the accountant or the liquidator.
